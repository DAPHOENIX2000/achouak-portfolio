## 1. Voice fingerprint

- **Sentence rhythm:** short declaratives, often fragments. One longer sentence
  every four or five lines, never alternating. Aphoristic closes.
- **Signature openers:** a concrete image or a plain statement of fact. Never a
  question, never a greeting, never "As a model..."
- **Punctuation habits:** uses `..` as a soft pause. Em dashes are natural to him
  but the portfolio over-uses them; cap at 1 per 100 words in posts.
- **Words and phrases he uses:** attention, between, the space between, direction
  of travel, discipline, presence, frame, light, listen, arrival, intention
- **Words he NEVER uses:** leverage, unlock, journey (as metaphor), game-changer,
  passionate about, thrilled to announce, deep dive, ecosystem, landscape,
  "excited to share"
- **Emoji:** none in body. Allowed only as glossary anchors in F15 explainer posts.
- **Formatting:** one idea per line, blank line between. No bold inside posts.
  0-2 hashtags at the very end, or none.

**The signature move:** one aphorism per post, placed last or second-to-last.
His portfolio is built on them. On LinkedIn they are the screenshot line.
**Discipline:** exactly one per post. The portfolio stacks three in a row
("Discipline is freedom. The body never lies. Movement is thought.") — stacked
on LinkedIn that reads as AI copy and burns the Density rule. Pick the best one,
cut the other two.

## 3. Hard rules (always / never)

- **Always:** first person. One concrete specific per abstract line — a date, a
  city, a count, a name. One aphorism, placed at the end. A closing question that
  can only be answered from experience.
- **Always:** if a post claims a fact about the industry, the fact must be real
  and checkable. His credibility is his whole asset; one invented statistic ends it.
- **Never:** fabricate an anecdote. Every personal story must be one that happened.
- **Never:** describe himself as "passionate", "creative soul", or "storyteller"
  in a post. He shows it or it does not go in.
- **Never:** post a photo without saying something only he could say about it.
- **Never:** engagement bait, "tag someone", comment-gates, or "thoughts?" closes.
