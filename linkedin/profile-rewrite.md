## 1. Headline

220 characters is the budget and almost nobody spends it. Format is
`[What you do] | [Who you help] [achieve what]`.

**Option A — the wedge, plainly stated (recommended)**

> Model & Creative Director in Shenzhen | Computer Science background | I help fashion, beauty and tech brands make images that hold up in the age of generative media | AR / EN / FR / 中文

**Option B — sharper, more provocative**

> I model, direct and shoot | CS degree before the camera, so I know exactly what the AI models can and cannot do | Fashion & tech brands, Shenzhen · Morocco · Global

**Option C — booking-first, least clever**

> Model · Photographer · Creative Director | Shenzhen-based, working China, Gulf & Europe | Editorial, campaign, motion | Computer science background, fluent in 4 languages

Take A. It is the only one that states the dual credential *and* names who it is
for. B is better writing and worse positioning. C ranks best for casting searches
and says nothing memorable, so use it only if bookings this quarter matter more
than the seat you are building.

**Search terms deliberately included:** model, creative director, photographer,
Shenzhen, fashion, campaign, editorial. Casting and brand teams search these.

## 9. Photo

Your portfolio has twenty frames stronger than most profile photos. The criteria
are narrower than editorial though: face fills about 60% of the frame, shot within
three years, natural light, eye contact, a slight smile. Several of the B&W
portraits will work with a tighter crop. Do not use the overexposed or
half-in-shadow frames here, however good they are — profile photos are read at
48 pixels.
