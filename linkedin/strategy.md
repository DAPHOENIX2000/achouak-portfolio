# LinkedIn Strategy — Achouak Yassine

## The wedge

Most models post photos. Most AI people post threads about models (the other
kind). Almost nobody is both, and nobody is both *while living in Shenzhen*.

That is the position: **the person who can stand in front of the camera and read
the code behind the one replacing him.** Computer science degree, then modeling,
photography, direction. Morocco to China. Arabic, French, English, Chinese.

Everything below is built to make that seat unmistakable within 90 days.

## Who you are talking to

| Tier | Who | Share of engagement | What you want from them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peers | Photographers, stylists, casting directors, agency creatives, other models | 70% | Referrals and the reflex to tag you |
| Aspirational | Creative directors, brand heads, virtual-production leads, AI-media founders | 20% | One repost from them beats a hundred likes |
| Prospects | Brand marketers booking talent in China / Gulf / Europe | 10% | Bookings and licensing conversations |

That 70/20/10 split is the *commenting* budget, not the posting one. See
`engagement-playbook.md`.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Why it works for you |

No pillar above 60% in a week. Never the same hook formula twice in seven days.

## On posting every day

You asked for daily. Here is the honest read, then the plan does what you decide.

The playbook's rule is 3-5 posts a week; 6+ triggers a cannibalization signal in
LinkedIn's ranker, where your own posts compete against each other and each one
lands softer. The thing that actually drives inbound is **comments on other
people's posts, 10-20 a day**. That is the daily habit worth having.

So the default plan is:

- **5 posts a week** (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri), weekends off
- **Engagement every single day**, weekends included

That is daily presence without the self-competition. If you want literal 7 posts
a week anyway, say so and I will extend the calendar. It is your account and the
downside is a softer reach curve, not a penalty.

## Cadence and timing

- Posting window: **7:30-9:00 AM** in the timezone of the audience you want that
  day. Shenzhen morning reaches China and the Gulf. For a Europe or Morocco
  facing post, publish 2:00-3:30 PM Shenzhen time.
- Tue/Wed/Thu carry the most weight. Put the AI x image posts there.
- Friday after 2 PM and weekends: comment, do not post.

## The 90-day arc

1. **Weeks 1-4, claim the seat.** Every AI x image post makes the dual
   credential unmissable. Profile rewritten first, so the traffic lands somewhere.
2. **Weeks 5-8, show the work.** Craft posts with real frames. Start the named
   Community posts, get tagged into other people's threads.
3. **Weeks 9-12, convert.** Soft offers: licensing point of view, creative
   direction availability, an inbound-ready Featured section.

## What we are not doing

- No engagement bait, no comment-gates, no "tag someone who needs this"
- No posting a photo with a caption any model could have written
- No invented statistics, ever. One fabricated number and the dual-credential
  position, which is the whole asset, is gone.
