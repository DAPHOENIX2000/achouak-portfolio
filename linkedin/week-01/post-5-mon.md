## Read this before you use it

Named gratitude is the highest-repost formula in the set, and it is the one that
turns peers into a referral network. It also cannot be written by anyone but you,
because the entire mechanism is **real names of real people who will see it.**

Generic gratitude ("grateful for my amazing team") is worse than posting nothing.

> Who gave you the correction you did not want?

## Rules for filling it

- **Three people maximum.** Five reads as a list, three reads as a debt.
- One concrete act each. If you cannot name what they did, they do not go in.
- Tag them properly so they get the notification. That is the repost mechanism.
- No "I would not be here without" and no "the amazing". Name the act, stop.
- This can be photographers, stylists, a casting director, a teacher from the CS
  years, whoever is true. It does not have to be all one profession, and mixing a
  professor in with two photographers actually makes the post better.
